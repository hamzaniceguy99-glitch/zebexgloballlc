Our services, in detail

Four services for US suppliers selling abroad, each quoted in writing before work begins.

Program 01 · Who you're dealing with

Finding & checking buyers

An enthusiastic enquiry from abroad is not a customer. We identify realistic buyers for the product, check company details, trading history and references, and tell you plainly when something doesn't stand up — which is cheaper than finding out after a container has shipped.

Program 02 · Agreed before shipping

Terms & payment

Most export disputes are terms disputes. We agree Incoterms explicitly so the point where cost and risk transfer is in writing, work through payment methods appropriate to the buyer and market, and make sure both sides have signed the same document in the same language.

Program 03 · Booked and tracked

Freight & shipping

Freight quotes are hard to compare because they include different things. We gather them on the same basis, explain what each covers, book with licensed carriers and forwarders, and keep the shipment tracked so you hear about a delay from us rather than from your customer.

Program 04 · Complete, first time

Export documentation

We prepare and cross-check the commercial invoice, packing list and supporting certificates for consistency, and hand a complete file to your licensed broker or forwarder. Export declarations and customs filings are made by licensed professionals, not by us, and compliance obligations stay with the exporter of record.
