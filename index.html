How we work

A Florida company, and the licensed parts stay licensed

Zebex Global LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We are not customs brokers, freight forwarders or attorneys. Customs entries and export declarations are filed by licensed professionals, and export controls, sanctions screening and product compliance remain the exporter's obligations. We'll raise those questions early and point you to the right adviser rather than guessing at the answer.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

We've had an enquiry from abroad. Is it real?

Often not, or not on the terms offered. Verification is the first thing we do and the cheapest step in the whole process.

Are you a customs broker?

No. We prepare and check documentation and work with your licensed broker. Filings are theirs.

How do we get paid safely?

It depends on the buyer, the market and the order size. We'll work through the options with you — the right answer for a first order is rarely open credit.

Do you take commission on sales?

Only if that's separately agreed in writing. The default is a quoted fee for the work, so our advice isn't bent toward closing a deal.

What about export controls?

Those obligations sit with the exporter. We'll flag when a product or destination needs specialist screening and point you to the right professional.

Can you guarantee the buyer pays?

No. We can verify who they are and structure terms that reduce the risk, which is what can honestly be offered.

Got an overseas enquiry?

A free consultation on the buyer, the market and whether it stands up.
